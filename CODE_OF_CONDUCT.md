# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive.

- Discuss the technical problem or product need, not the person reporting it.
- Do not publish another person's personal information, credentials, or private correspondence.
- Do not use issues for spam, promotion, harassment, or coordinated abuse.
- Give maintainers enough evidence to reproduce a report without exposing sensitive data.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates these expectations or creates a privacy or security risk.

Serious conduct concerns should be sent through [Vedika support](https://vedika.io/contact), not debated in a public issue.
